# Speaking — w12: A Closer Look at Diligence

Model answers for reading and review. Personal stories and examples are illustrative, not real events.

## 1. Why is Diligence just as crucial as Delegation, Description, and Discernment in professional engineering workflows?

**Answer:**  
Delegation, description and discernment help us get good results quickly, so they are mostly about effectiveness and efficiency. Diligence covers the ethical and safety side: data protection, honesty and responsibility. In professional work, a fast result is not enough if it leaks private data, contains hidden mistakes or breaks company rules. Without diligence, the other three Ds can cause harm very efficiently.

**Example:**  
A script may save hours of work, but if it sends customer data to a public AI service, the company may have a serious problem.

---

## 2. How do you practice Creation Diligence to ensure sensitive company code or API credentials never leak to public models?

**Answer:**  
Creation diligence means I think carefully about which AI system I use and what data I give it. Before I share code, I check the service's data policy and my organization's rules. I never paste passwords, API keys or private configuration into a prompt. I keep secrets in environment files that are never committed, and I replace real values with placeholders when I ask for help.

**Example:**  
Instead of pasting my real router configuration, I replace the passwords and public IP addresses with "XXX". The AI can still help, and nothing sensitive leaves my computer.

---

## 3. Why is being forthright about AI collaboration through Transparency Diligence important for maintaining team trust?

**Answer:**  
Transparency diligence means being open and honest about AI's role with everyone who needs to know. Colleagues have the right to know which parts of the work were AI-assisted. When they know, they can review those parts carefully and trust the rest of the work. If they find out about the AI use later by themselves, they may start to doubt everything I have done.

**Example:**  
When I share a pull request, I write which files the AI generated and which ones I wrote myself. The reviewer then knows where to look more carefully.

---

## 4. How does Deployment Diligence ensure that you can confidently stand behind every script, configuration, or article you share?

**Answer:**  
Deployment diligence means I take responsibility for anything I use or share after I create it with AI. Before I share a script, a configuration or an article, I verify the facts, test the code and check for mistakes and usage rights. The standard is the same as if I had written everything myself. When I have checked everything, I can confidently stand behind it.

**Example:**  
Before I publish a router script, I run it on a test device first. Before I publish an article, I check every fact against the original source.

---

## 5. Explain how you would draft a Diligence Statement for an open-source tool where AI was instrumental in scaffolding code.

**Answer:**  
A diligence statement is a short, honest note about how AI was used and who is responsible for the result. For an open-source tool, I would name the AI system and explain that it was instrumental in scaffolding the code, for example the project structure and the first version of the functions. Then I would describe my review: I read, tested and changed the code myself. Finally, I would say clearly that I take full responsibility for the code, and I would put the statement in the README.

**Example:**  
"This tool was scaffolded with the help of Claude. All code was reviewed and tested by the maintainer, who is fully responsible for it."
