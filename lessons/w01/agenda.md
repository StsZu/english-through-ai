## Learning contract

I am the student, but I want to lead this course. Here is what I ask from you.

**What I will do:**
- Prepare the material before every lesson (video, transcript, glossary, exercises)
- Speak for most of the lesson
- Explain the concepts to you, not the other way round
- Record my talks and track my errors in a repository

**What I ask you to do:**
- Correct my English, not my ideas
- Interrupt me when I make the same mistake twice
- Ask hard follow-up questions so I have to explain again in other words
- Refuse to switch to Ukrainian except in the error block (10–20 min)

**Language rule:** English only, except minutes 10–20.

## Block 2 (05–15) — Talk

**Question:** What do you know about AI?
**Rules:** 3 minutes, no notes, no slides.

Please listen for:
- verb tenses
- articles (a / the / zero)
- word order in longer sentences
- pauses longer than 3 seconds

I have already recorded a baseline version of this talk. Please tell me
after the lesson whether the live version was better or worse.

## Block 6 (55–75) — Live lab: model bake-off

I will run the same prompt in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok, live,
and describe the differences in English as I go.

**Prompt I will use:**

```
Explain the difference between "effective" and "efficient"
to a B1 English learner. Give 3 examples from working with AI tools.
```

I will comment on:
- which answer is easiest for a B1 learner to understand
- which one is too long
- which one invented something
- which one I would actually use in my glossary

Please correct the language I use to describe the comparison. I expect to
need words like *concise, verbose, accurate, useful, misleading*.
