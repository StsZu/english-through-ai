# Speaking — w10: A Closer Look at Discernment

Model answers for reading and review. Personal stories and examples are illustrative, not real events.

## 1. Why is Discernment considered the flip side of Description? How do they form a continuous feedback loop in your technical work?

**Answer:**  
Description is about clearly communicating what you want, and discernment is about deciding whether what you get back meets your needs. That is why discernment is called the flip side of description: they are the two halves of one conversation. Together, they form a loop: I describe the task, evaluate the result, improve my description and try again. Each round brings the result closer to what I really need.

**Example:**  
I ask an agent for a script that checks my router's uptime. It prints too much information, so I ask it to show only one line, and the next version is exactly right.

---

## 2. How do you apply product discernment to catch subtle hallucinations or syntax shortcomings in code generated by an AI assistant?

**Answer:**  
Product discernment means judging the quality of the output itself: is it accurate, correct and useful for my goal? With code, I never trust it just because it looks clean. I read it, run it, test unusual cases and check every unfamiliar function in the official documentation. Subtle hallucinations often hide in function names, parameters or library versions.

**Example:**  
An AI once used a command option that does not exist in RouterOS. The syntax looked perfect, but the router returned an error, and the documentation showed the correct option.

## 3. Describe an experience where an AI model experienced a lapse in attention or fell into circular reasoning. How did process discernment help you fix it?

**Answer:**  
In a long debugging session, an AI suggested the same fix three times, even after I said it did not work. It was stuck in circular reasoning and could not consider any other cause. Process discernment helped me see that the problem was not one answer but the AI's whole approach. So I stopped the loop, summarized what we already knew and asked it for completely different possible causes. Sometimes it is better to start a new conversation with a clean summary.

---

## 4. When evaluating an interactive session, how does performance discernment help you adjust the AI's communication style and verbosity?

**Answer:**  
Performance discernment means I evaluate how the AI communicates with me, not only what it produces. I ask myself: is it too long or too short, does it ask too many questions, and does it respond well to my feedback? If its style does not help me, I give clear instructions to change it. This makes the interaction more efficient for both of us.

**Example:**  
When an agent writes long explanations for small changes, I say: "Be brief. Show only the changed lines and one sentence of explanation."

---

## 5. Why is human oversight an irreplaceable safeguard in production systems? When should poor AI results cause you to revisit delegation decisions?

**Answer:**  
Even the most advanced AI can make reasoning errors or factual mistakes, or act in unexpected ways. In production systems, these mistakes affect real users, so human oversight is the last safeguard. Usually, when discernment finds a problem, better description is the solution. But if the results stay poor after several clear attempts, I should revisit delegation: maybe I am using the wrong tool, or this task should not go to AI at all.

**Example:**  
If an AI keeps making mistakes with complex firewall logic, I may decide to write that part myself and only use the AI to document it.
