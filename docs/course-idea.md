# English Through AI: Learning the Language by Creating Practical Projects

## Course Idea

This course combines English learning with the practical study of artificial intelligence. Instead of learning English through traditional textbook topics, learners explore AI technologies, compare tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Grok, and use them to create real projects.

Each lesson has two connected goals: developing English communication skills and learning how to use AI effectively and responsibly. Learners read authentic materials, study relevant vocabulary, discuss new technologies, evaluate AI-generated responses, and explain their findings in English.

The course follows a project-based approach. Learners use AI to create lesson materials, presentations, vocabulary exercises, quizzes, audio scripts, research summaries, and other useful products. These projects provide meaningful opportunities to practise speaking, reading, writing, listening, grammar, and pronunciation.

The teacher helps learners improve their English, understand complex ideas, verify information, recognise unreliable AI output, and communicate their conclusions clearly. AI supports the learning process, but human judgement, creativity, and critical thinking remain essential.

By the end of the course, learners will be able to:

- discuss artificial intelligence confidently in English;
- write clear and effective prompts;
- compare different AI platforms;
- work with texts, documents, images, audio, and research materials;
- evaluate the accuracy and usefulness of AI-generated content;
- explain technical concepts in accessible language;
- create and present a complete practical project in English.

The final result will be both improved English proficiency and a portfolio of practical AI-assisted projects, including a learner-created mini-course.
